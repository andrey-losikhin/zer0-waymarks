# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code, not the person presenting them.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it with useful context.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and safety concerns.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained disruption,
personal attacks, and publishing private information are not tolerated in project
spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban
participants whose behavior is harmful or inappropriate. Reports should be sent
privately to the maintainer through the security contact described in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as reasonably possible.

This policy applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other public communication made on behalf of the project.
